Mirror Delay Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Mirror Delay is a delay-mirror training app for iOS, created by Kaoru Okamoto, an independent developer based in Japan. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short, Mirror Delay is built to work without collecting your data.

Data collection

Mirror Delay does not collect, store, or share your personal data. It has no user accounts or sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. This applies to everyone who uses the app. Because no personal data is collected, there is none for you to access, correct, or delete.

Analytics provided by Apple

Like all App Store apps, Mirror Delay is covered by Apple's standard App Analytics. If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in your device's iOS settings, Apple may give the developer aggregate, anonymized statistics about app usage and crashes. This information is collected and anonymized by Apple, is controlled by your device settings, and does not identify you. The developer receives no other information about you.

Camera and microphone

Mirror Delay uses your device's camera to show a live and time-delayed view of yourself, like a mirror. The camera image is processed only on your device, in memory, to produce the delayed view. The app does not record, save, or upload your camera video. It does not use the microphone and captures no audio.

Pairing with a nearby device

Mirror Delay can mirror the camera view to a second nearby device. When you use this feature, the video is sent directly between the two paired devices over an encrypted connection — a local network or a direct device-to-device link. It does not pass through any server run by the developer.

Purchases

Starting with version 1.1.0, Mirror Delay has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The developer does not collect payment information through the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
