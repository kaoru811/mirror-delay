Common questions

The camera does not start.
Open the iOS Settings app, find Mirror Delay, and make sure camera access is allowed.

Are all features free?
Yes. Two-Device Mirror, Landscape, Interval Timer, Zoom, and camera switching are included at no charge. No account or subscription is required. If you see an Unlock Plus screen, update Mirror Delay to version 1.1.0 or later.

Where can I check a past Mirror Delay Plus charge or request a refund?
Sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Follow Apple’s instructions to check your purchase history or request a refund.

Pairing with another device does not connect.
Update Mirror Delay on both devices to the latest version (1.1.0 or later). Version 1.0.3 cannot connect to this version. Then make sure both devices have Mirror Delay open and are near each other, that Wi-Fi is enabled on both devices, and that local network access is allowed for Mirror Delay in iOS Settings. A shared Wi-Fi network or internet connection is not required. If it still does not connect, turn off any VPN that may block local connections, and keep both devices unlocked while connecting.
