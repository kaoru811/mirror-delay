Mirror Delay Support

Last updated: May 25, 2026

For help, feedback, bug reports, or feature requests, contact kaoruokamoto0811@gmail.com. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Common questions

The camera does not start.
Open the iOS Settings app, find Mirror Delay, and make sure camera access is allowed.

I subscribed to Mirror Delay Plus, but it is not active.
Open Settings in the app, tap Unlock Plus, and then tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Plus screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, and select Mirror Delay.

Pairing with another device does not connect.
Make sure both devices have Mirror Delay open and are near each other, that Wi-Fi is turned on and both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that local network access is allowed for Mirror Delay in iOS Settings. If it still does not connect, turn off any VPN that may block local connections, and keep both devices unlocked while connecting.
