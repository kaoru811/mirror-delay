Utsushi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Utsushi is a copy practice app for iOS, created by Kaoru Okamoto, an independent developer based in Japan. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short, Utsushi is built to work without collecting your data.

Data collection

Utsushi does not collect, store, or share your personal data. It has no user accounts or sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. It never sends anything over the network — nothing you do in Utsushi leaves your device. This applies to everyone who uses the app. Because no personal data is collected, there is none for you to access, correct, or delete.

Analytics provided by Apple

Like all App Store apps, Utsushi is covered by Apple's standard App Analytics. If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in your device's iOS settings, Apple may give the developer aggregate, anonymized statistics about app usage and crashes. This information is collected and anonymized by Apple, is controlled by your device settings, and does not identify you. The developer receives no other information about you.

Camera and microphone

Utsushi uses your device's camera and microphone to record short clips of your movement, with sound, so you can watch each attempt back right after you make it. Recordings are kept only in temporary storage on your device and are replaced as you keep practicing. They are not saved to your photo library, and they are never uploaded anywhere.

Photo library

Utsushi asks for access to your photo library so you can pick a video to use as the model. The clip you choose is copied into the app's temporary storage for playback. If a video or its thumbnail is stored in iCloud rather than on your device, iOS downloads it from your iCloud Photos; this transfer is handled entirely by Apple. Utsushi only reads from your library — it does not add, change, or delete anything in it, and it does not save recordings there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
