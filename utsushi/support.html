For help, feedback, bug reports, or feature requests, contact kaoruokamoto0811@gmail.com. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Common questions

The camera does not start, or recordings have no sound.
Open the iOS Settings app, find Utsushi, and make sure camera and microphone access are allowed.

My videos do not appear in the list.
Open the iOS Settings app, find Utsushi, and make sure photo access is allowed. If you allowed access to only some videos, add the video you want to the selection, or allow full library access.

Are my recordings saved anywhere?
No. Each recording is played back once in the loop and kept only temporarily on your device. Recordings are not saved to your photo library, and nothing is uploaded.

How do I practice with my own model video?
Tap a video in the list, set the trim handles around the part you want to copy, and tap Start. The loop plays the model, records you for the same length, and replays your attempt — over and over until you tap to stop.
